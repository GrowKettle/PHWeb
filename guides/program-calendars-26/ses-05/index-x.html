Session 5: Stop Doing It All Yourself - Tuesday, 8/18/26, 7 PM CDT

We continue with the second of three sessions in the "Build the Experience" section of the webinar series, Session 5: Stop Doing It All Yourself.

Units often revolve around one or two key people. They know how to get things done. They matter. But they also create a vulnerability. Someday they'll leave. By sharing responsibility and ownership, the leader creates owners among the unit's other leaders, making the unit stronger and more sustainable.

The downloadable Responsibility Map structures a converations toward creating shared ownership.

  • Key Insight: Sustainability comes from shared ownership.
  • Focus: Parent roles, distributing responsibility.
  • Outcome: Reduced leader stress and increased engagement.
  • Action Step: Move toward ownership with one meaninful responsibility.
