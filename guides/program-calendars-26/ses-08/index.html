Session 8: What Strong Units Do Differently - Tuesday, 10/6/26, 7 PM CDT

Session 8, Don't Lose It Once It Starts continues the Building Strong Units Through Program Calendars webinar series.

Strong units survive because leaders intentionally develop the people who will carry the culture forward. They don't wait for leadership vacancies to begin developing future leaders and owners.

Culture does not survive automatically. Culture is always one leadership generation away from extinction. Culture must be continually renewed, not merely preserved.

The downloadable Ownership Pipeline Assessment toolis a practical four-step diagnostic to help your Key 3 build a bridge to the next generation of leaders

  • Key Insight: Culture is always one leadership generation away from extinction.
  • Focus: Development of future leaders and owners.
  • Outcome: Units maintain momentum and focus.
  • Action Step: Pick the one area where your unit's culture is most at risk due to single-leader dependency.

    A calendar, exciting activities, advancement, and regular communications are all critical. However, without consistent execution, a unit will falter. This recordings shows how to maintain momentum through simple, consistent execution habits.
