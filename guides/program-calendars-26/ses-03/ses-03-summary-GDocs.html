Predictability Builds Trust

Kettle Country District Program Calendar Webinar, Session 3, 7/21/26

Summary

In Session 1, A Calendar is a Promise, we painted the big picture: the calendar is a promise to families. Session 2, Let the Calendar Build Itself, showed how starting with events that are unchangeable, the calendar practically builds itself.

Last Tuesday, during Session 3, we talked about identifying the rhythms that are in all Scouting units. Once families begin to recognize those rhythms, they begin to understand what they can expect, what comes next, and how they can participate. When people understand what to expect, trust has a place to grow. Predictability is not sameness, and it is not removing variety. Predictability is the ability to recognize a rhythm, and that is a significant difference.

Families don’t join units for events, they join for experiences.

We introduced the Rhythm Map, a tool to help unit leaders see their units’ rhythms in eight areas. The Anchor Calendar from Session 2 asks, ‘What is shaping our year?’ The Rhythm Map asks, ‘What rhythms are shaping our program experience?’ Those are different questions, and we need both of them.

How would we describe our unit’s rhythm? Is the rhythm strong, inconsistent, accidental, or missing? What should we strengthen? Those questions are practical because they force leaders to look at the actual experience families and Scouts are receiving, not the experience we hope they are receiving or the experience we meant to create. For example, a pack can have a calendar full of activities and still feel unpredictable. The Rhythm Map helps leaders step back from the activity list and examine the rhythms underneath it. The purpose of the map is not to fix everything at once but to pick one rhythm to strengthen.

How do repeated activities become more than isolated events? A calendar provides structure. Rhythms create predictability. But a strong program requires connection. We’ll talk about that for half an hour on Tuesday, 8/4/26 at 7 PM CDT during Session 4, Random Events to Real Program.
