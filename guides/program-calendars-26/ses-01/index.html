Session 1: A Calendar Is a Promise - Tuesday, 6/23/26, 7 PM CDT

What if your unit’s program felt clear, predictable—and something scouts, volunteers, and families looked forward to?

This summer, Kettle Country is launching a short webinar series designed to help every pack, troop, crew, and ship build a clear, predictable program calendar that works for everyone.

  • Key Insight: Strong programs are designed through predictable structure.
  • Focus: Why calendars drive retention and belonging; overview of the 6-12-18 month approach, introduction to 90-day starting calendar.
  • Outcome: Leaders understand a calendar's importance and gain the confidence to begin.
  • Action Step: Draft 90 day calendar.
