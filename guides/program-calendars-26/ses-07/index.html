Session 7: Don't Lose It Once It Starts - Tuesday, 9/22/26, 7 PM CDT

Session 7, Don't Lose It Once It Starts continues the Building Strong Units Through Program Calendars webinar series.

Strong programs are sustained through simple, consistent execution habits, not constant bursts of effort. We constantly confuse having a plan on paper with executing a program in the room. Execution is where programs succeed or fail.

The downloadable Unit Habit Audit tool helps leaders discover the behaviors that are shaping their unit and identify opportunities to strengthen consistent execution over time.

  • Key Insight: Execution is where programs succeed or fail.
  • Focus: Weekly use of calendar, communication rhythm.
  • Outcome: Confident execution.
  • Action Step: Establish monthly communication rhythm.

  • Video Recording of Session 7 (24:34)

    A calendar, exciting activities, advancement, and regular communications are all critical. However, without consistent execution, a unit will falter. This recordings shows how to maintain momentum through simple, consistent execution habits.

    Execution is where programs succeed or fail.
